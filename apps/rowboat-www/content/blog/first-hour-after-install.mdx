Signed installers ship for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows, and Linux. The page can recommend a file from your browser. You can still pick another. Both paths hit GitHub Releases.

On first run you choose: sign in to Oppulence, or bring your own model keys and stay local.

Then, if you want the register: connect Google. Slack and HubSpot can wait. Point the vault at a folder you already have, or let the app create one.

## What not to expect in hour one

A full year of mail. Slack DMs. Salesforce. Native meeting capture on Windows. Those are either out of the current connector set, or they are a different recording path.

The install walkthrough is [this guide](/guides/install-oppulence). The files are on [download](/download).
