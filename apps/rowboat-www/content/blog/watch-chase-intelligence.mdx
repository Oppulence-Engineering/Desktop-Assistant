The public price list is three names.

**Watch** is free. A 90-day report of what you owe, with source links. It is the first pass, not the live register.

**Chase** is $99 a month. At-risk promises and approved follow-ups. This is the plan for when the register is part of the week.

**Intelligence** is $249 a month. Change summaries and exportable records on top of Chase. Useful for renewals, escalations, and handovers.

## What we are not selling

There is no seat tax on the published list. There is no annual discount listed because we have not published one. There is no enterprise SKU hiding in a popup.

Upgrade when the habit exists. The comparison lives on [pricing](/pricing).
