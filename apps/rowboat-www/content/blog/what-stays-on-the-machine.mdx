There are three pipes, on purpose.

**Local.** The Markdown vault, meeting audio, on-device Whisper transcripts, local embeddings, and `~/.rowboat/` config. Delete the folder and those copies are gone.

**Cloud ledger.** When you sign in, relationship state syncs so web and desktop share the same register. Account identity goes through WorkOS.

**Models.** Bring-your-own keys go to the provider you configured. Signed-in hosted calls go through the credit-gated gateway. Prompts can contain relationship context you asked the assistant to use.

Analytics is PostHog, fail-closed until you enable it. Native crash dumps stay on the machine.

## The honest local-only setup

Bring your own model keys. Keep the vault on disk. Do not sign in. That is a real configuration, and it is not the same as the signed-in ledger.
