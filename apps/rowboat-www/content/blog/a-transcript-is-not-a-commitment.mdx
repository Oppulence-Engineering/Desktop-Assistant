Most meeting tools join as a participant. Oppulence Desktop records the room you are already in. The file lives on your disk. Transcription can run without uploading the audio.

On macOS 14.2 and later, a native sidecar records microphone and system audio. Other platforms use a renderer fallback. That difference is real. We will not advertise native capture as a Windows feature.

## You can keep the recorder you already like

Desktop can pull Granola and Fireflies notes into the local vault. Oppulence is not asking you to replace a recorder. It is asking you to keep the agreement after the transcript folder goes stale.

Voice is a separate dictation and meeting app. It is not the ledger. They are different downloads.
